Dress Code

Dress to Kill

Black, red, and a little bit dangerous. You may arrive as:

  • The Suspect: dress for the way you did it. Poisoner, strangler, candlestick in the conservatory: let your outfit be the murder weapon.
  • The Detective: trench coat, magnifying glass, an air of suspicion.
  • The Victim: dress for the way you met your end. Poisoned, pushed, done in by the chandelier: arrive looking fabulous and suitably deceased.
  • Yourself: festive attire is always welcome.

Bonus points for accessories. We hear is in fashion this season.

A word from the detectives: dress the part, but leave the weapons at home. No real or prop weapons of any kind. Anything that could hurt a fellow dancer will be confiscated at the door. Suggest the crime with your costume (a smudge of fake blood, a label reading "poison") instead.

Rules of the House

Good to Know

  • Footwear: soft-soled dance shoes or socks only. Street shoes are not allowed on the Roble Gym floor. Investigators found .
  • No weapons, real or prop: no knives, swords, toy guns, or anything sharp or heavy. Our detectives will confiscate them at the door, and you can collect them on your way out. Keep everyone on the dance floor in one piece.
  • No partner needed: come solo or with friends. Partners change all night.
  • Questions: email the committee about tickets, volunteering, or accessibility.
